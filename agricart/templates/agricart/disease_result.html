{% if disease == "Brownspot" %}

🟤 Brown Spot Disease

Cause

Caused by the fungus Bipolaris oryzae. It develops in nutrient-deficient soils and humid conditions.

Symptoms

  • Small brown circular spots on leaves
  • Yellow halo around spots
  • Reduced grain quality

Treatment / Cure

  • Spray fungicides like Mancozeb or Carbendazim
  • Apply balanced fertilizers (NPK)

Prevention

  • Use disease-resistant varieties
  • Avoid nutrient deficiency
  • Ensure proper drainage
{% elif disease == "Blast" %}

🟤 Rice Blast Disease

Cause

Caused by the fungus Magnaporthe oryzae.

Symptoms

  • Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves
  • Gray or white center with dark border

Treatment

  • Apply Tricyclazole fungicide
  • Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer

Prevention

  • Use resistant varieties
  • Maintain proper spacing

{% elif disease == "dead_heart" %}

🟤 Dead Heart Disease

Cause

Caused by larvae of the insect Scirpophaga incertulas (yellow stem borer) which bore into the rice stem.

Symptoms

  • Central shoot dries and turns brown
  • Dead central tiller that can be easily pulled out
  • No grain formation in affected tillers

Treatment / Cure

  • Apply insecticides like Chlorantraniliprole or Cartap Hydrochloride
  • Remove and destroy infected tillers

Prevention

  • Use resistant rice varieties
  • Maintain field sanitation
  • Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer
{% elif disease == "Rice Hispa" %}

🟤 Rice Hispa Disease

Cause

Caused by the insect Dicladispa armigera. Adults scrape leaves and larvae mine inside them.

Symptoms

  • White streaks on leaves
  • Leaves appear skeletonized
  • Reduced plant growth

Treatment / Cure

  • Spray Chlorpyrifos or Quinalphos
  • Remove heavily infected leaves

Prevention

  • Keep field weed-free
  • Timely planting and proper spacing
  • Monitor fields regularly
{% elif disease == "Tungro" %}

🟤 Rice Tungro Disease

Cause

Caused by Rice Tungro Virus, transmitted by green leafhopper insects.

Symptoms

  • Yellow to orange discoloration of leaves
  • Stunted plant growth
  • Reduced number of tillers

Treatment / Cure

  • Control leafhoppers using Imidacloprid
  • Remove infected plants immediately

Prevention

  • Use virus-free seedlings
  • Control insect vectors regularly
  • Grow resistant rice varieties
